UK Radio - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

UK Radio ("the app") lets you listen to live UK radio stations and podcasts. This policy explains what information the app uses. By using the app you agree to this policy.

Information we do not collect

The app does not ask you to create an account. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number or precise location.

Information stored on your device

Your favourite stations and podcasts, recently played items and your light/dark choice are saved only on your device. They are not sent to us. Clearing the app data removes them.

Information shared with third parties

  • Radio streams: when you play a station or programme, your device connects to the server of that station or broadcaster. They can see your IP address and basic connection details, as with any website. We do not control these servers; their own policies apply.
  • Podcasts: when you open a podcast, the app downloads its episode list (RSS feed) from the podcast publisher, and episodes play from the publisher's server. They can see your IP address.
  • Fonts: the app loads fonts from Google Fonts, which can see your IP address.
  • Advertising: the app may show ads (banner and full-screen ads) provided by Google AdMob. These services may collect and use device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), your IP address and how you interact with ads, to show and measure ads. You can reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalisation in your Android settings. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

We do not keep personal data on our own servers. Streams use HTTPS where the broadcaster supports it; some stations only offer unencrypted (http) streams.

Your choices

You can clear the app data, reset your advertising ID in Android settings, or uninstall the app at any time.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available in the app (menu, Privacy Policy) and at the link on our Google Play page.
